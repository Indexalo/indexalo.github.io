Ask for it. We build it.

Your business isn't a template, so the service isn't either. When you need a new report, a connection, a check or a feature the app doesn't have yet, you ask, we agree with you what it takes, and our team builds it.

  • You ask: Which ads actually turn into paying customers?

    We build: Report

    Ad spend joined to your sales pipeline, with the cost at every step from lead to closed sale.

  • You ask: Can each area manager open straight to their own area?

    We build: Feature

    A page per area with its own filters, so every manager starts from their own numbers.

  • You ask: Tell us when something's off, not at month-end.

    We build: Alert

    An alert to email or Slack for the numbers you watch: a slow day, spend over budget, data that stopped arriving.

  • You ask: Our numbers don't match the accounting system.

    We build: Check

    A check that compares the two and flags every difference, so you know which figure to trust.

  • You ask: I need to read what the customer actually said.

    We build: Feature

    A searchable log of every enquiry, with the conversation one click away.

  • You ask: Commissions take days to work out.

    We build: Report

    A commission report on your own pay rules, worked out from the same numbers as everything else.

Examples of the kind of request we take on. If something isn't a good fit, we say so up front.

Questions, answered.

Something else? Ask us directly.

Do we need anyone technical?

No. You tell us what you want to see and give us access to the systems your numbers live in. Connecting them, defining the numbers and building the reports is our job.

What do you need from us?

Access to your systems, and time on a call to tell us how the business runs and what you want to see. After that, we go through the first reports with you.

Which systems can you connect?

Ready-made connectors cover Salesforce, Shopify, Google Ads, Meta Ads, Google Analytics, Klaviyo and Google Business Profile, among others. Other systems, our team connects for you: your ERP, accounting or point of sale, a booking or enquiry system, an in-house database, a spreadsheet. Which ones, we agree when we plan your setup.

Can we ask for changes, new reports or something custom?

Yes. Questions change, and the reports change with them. If you need a new connection, a check or a feature the app doesn't have yet, tell us: we agree with you what it takes, then build it. If a request isn't a good fit, we say so up front.

Where do we see the reports?

In the Indexalo app, in your browser. Exports can run on a schedule, alerts go to email or Slack, and tables export to CSV.

Who do we talk to when a number looks wrong?

The team that built your reports. We check it against your systems and fix it.

How long until we see our first reports?

It depends on which systems you use. After the call we come back with a plan: what we connect first and which reports come first.
