Questions, answered.

Something else? Ask us directly.

Which systems does Indexalo connect to?

Built in: Salesforce, Outreach, Shopify, Klaviyo, Google Business Profile, GA4, Search Console, Google Ads and Meta Ads, plus your own BigQuery tables and Indexalo surveys. Your ERP, accounting, point-of-sale and other systems, our team connects for you; which ones, we agree when we plan your setup.

Which teams is Indexalo for?

Sales, operations and finance, and anyone else who needs the numbers: sales by location, stock and suppliers, margins, cash and invoices, in one place.

Do you host the data, or can we use our own warehouse?

Either. We host the warehouse for you, or you connect your own BigQuery and we work in it.

Do we have to define our own metrics?

No. Defining your data is one of the hardest parts, so our team does it for you: what counts as revenue, a return or a late order. You approve the definitions, and dashboards, Ask AI and MCP then all use the same ones. The Semantic Layer that holds them is in beta.

Do I need a data team?

No. We connect your systems, host the warehouse and define the metrics for you. Then anyone on the team can ask in plain language: in the app, or in Claude and ChatGPT (beta).

Can I use Indexalo in Claude or ChatGPT?

In beta, through Indexalo MCP. During early access we connect Indexalo to your team's Claude or ChatGPT, and you ask there: the answers come from the same numbers and definitions as your dashboards.
