Questions, answered.

Something else? Ask us directly.

Which tools does Indexalo connect to?

Google Ads, Meta Ads, GA4, Search Console, PageSpeed, Shopify, Klaviyo, Salesforce, Outreach and Google Business Profile, plus your own BigQuery tables and Indexalo surveys. Using something we don't connect yet? Tell us on the demo call.

Why cost per order and not ROAS?

Neither Google Ads nor Meta sends order value, so a ROAS figure from them is a guess. Indexalo compares what each channel spent with the orders that followed, and measures that against your target.

Do I need a data team?

No. You connect accounts from their own login screens, the dashboards are ready-made, and Ask AI answers in plain language.

Can I use Indexalo in Claude or ChatGPT?

In beta, through Indexalo MCP. During early access we connect Indexalo to your team's Claude or ChatGPT, and you ask there: the answers come from the same numbers and definitions as your dashboards.
