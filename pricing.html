Pricing questions.

Something else? Ask us directly.

Which plan do I need?

If your numbers live in the systems Indexalo connects itself, such as Salesforce, Shopify or your own BigQuery, Starter. If you also need your accounting or point-of-sale system, Starter with one system our team connects. With an ERP, or two or three systems to join, Business. For many systems, or ones that need more than a standard connection, Tailored. We confirm the fit with you on the demo call.

What counts as a system our team connects?

A system the app doesn't connect on its own, such as an ERP, an accounting package or a point-of-sale system. Our team builds the connection for you. An ERP counts at a higher rate because it takes more work to connect. The built-in connectors don't count.

Can we ask our data in Claude or ChatGPT?

Yes, in beta, on every plan. During early access our team connects Indexalo to your team’s Claude or ChatGPT, with the same numbers and definitions as your dashboards. It is free while in beta, and the proposal is to keep it included on every plan.

Do you charge per user?

No. Every plan includes your whole team, so anyone can ask.

Is there a setup fee?

Starter on the built-in connectors has a guided setup at no extra cost. Connecting a system for you starts with a one-time setup project: from $1,000 on Starter and from $2,500 on Business, quoted after the demo call.

How much can we ask Ask AI?

Starter includes 500 questions a month and Business 2,000; Tailored is scoped with you. These allowances are proposals.

Are the other beta features included?

The Semantic Layer and Activation are in early access and free while they are in beta. On Business and Tailored our team writes your definitions, and the Semantic Layer is included; any plan can request it, and Activation. Their price at general release isn’t decided yet.

Monthly or yearly?

Either. Billed yearly, you pay for 10 months and get 12: two months free, a 17% saving. The switch above the plans shows both. Payment terms are set out in your order form.

Can I start without a call?

Not yet. For now every plan starts with a 30-minute demo call, and we reply within 24 hours to find a time.

What about a separate warehouse or BI dashboards of our own?

That's consulting, separate from any plan: a separate warehouse (such as Snowflake), pipelines, and Power BI, Tableau or Looker Studio dashboards, quoted as a project.
