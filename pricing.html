Consulting

Pricing questions.

Something else? Ask us directly.

Which plan do I need?

If your numbers live in the systems Indexalo connects itself, such as Salesforce, Shopify or your own BigQuery, Starter. If you also need your ERP, accounting or point-of-sale system in the same place, Business. For many systems, or ones that need more than a standard connection, Tailored. We confirm the fit with you on the demo call.

What counts as a system our team connects?

A system the app doesn't connect on its own, such as an ERP, an accounting package or a point-of-sale system. Our team builds the connection and maintains it while you're on the plan. The built-in connectors don't count.

Do you charge per user?

No. Every plan includes your whole team, so anyone can ask.

Is there a setup fee?

Business and Tailored start with a setup project: connecting your systems and writing your definitions. We quote it once, after the demo call. Starter's setup is agreed on the demo call.

Is Ask AI included?

Yes, on every plan, under a fair-use allowance.

Are the beta features included?

Semantic Layer, Activation and MCP are in early access and free while they are in beta. On Business and Tailored the Semantic Layer is included, because it holds the definitions we write for you; any plan can request the others. Their price at general release isn't decided yet.

Can I start without a call?

No. Every plan starts with a 30-minute demo call, and we reply within 24 hours to find a time.

How is billing set up?

The billing period and payment terms are set out in your order form, agreed after the demo call.

What about a data warehouse or BI dashboards of our own?

That's consulting, separate from any plan: warehouses on BigQuery or Snowflake, pipelines, and Power BI, Tableau or Looker Studio dashboards, quoted as a project.
